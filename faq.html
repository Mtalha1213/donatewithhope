Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Can I donate Zakat?+

Yes. Our Food for a Month campaign is 100% Zakat eligible, and our Medical Support and Emergency Appeals campaigns accept both Zakat and Sadaqah. Each campaign on our Campaigns page is clearly labeled with its eligibility.

Can I donate Sadaqah?+

Yes. Sadaqah is accepted across all four of our campaigns, and our Student Support campaign is specifically structured as 100% Sadaqah.

How are beneficiaries selected?+

Cases are reviewed individually before support is approved, based on genuine need and, where relevant, supporting documentation (such as medical records for the Medical Support campaign or enrollment status for Student Support). We aim to verify every case on the ground before funds are allocated.

How will I receive confirmation?+

Once our donation system is fully connected, you'll receive an automatic confirmation for every donation. Until then, you're welcome to email us directly and we'll confirm your gift personally.

How are donations used?+

Donations go toward the specific campaign and tier you choose — for example, a "Feed a Family for 14 Days" donation goes toward that food package. We publish updates on campaign progress on our Transparency page as campaigns move forward.

Still have a question?

Reach out directly and we'll get back to you personally — we're a small team and read every message.
