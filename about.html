Built on Trust, Driven by Compassion

We started with a simple question: how do we make sure a donor's generosity reaches the person who needs it — visibly, verifiably, every time?

Our Story

From one family's kitchen to a nationwide network

Donate With Hope Foundation began in 2016 when a small group of friends in Lahore started pooling funds to cover a neighbor's hospital bill. What followed was a food drive during a flood, then a scholarship for a promising student who couldn't afford school fees.

A decade later, that same instinct — verify the need, deliver the help, show the receipt — still shapes everything we do. We've grown into a foundation supporting families across Pakistan, but we've kept the part that made people trust us in the first place: nothing moves without a name, a story, and a paper trail.

Mission

What we do, every day

To connect generous donors around the world with verified charitable causes in Pakistan, delivering food, healthcare, education, and emergency relief through complete transparency and accountability.

Vision

Where we're headed

A future where no family in Pakistan has to choose between a meal and medicine — and where every donor can see, in plain terms, the difference their gift made.

Core Values

The four things we won't compromise on

Transparency

Every donation is traceable, every expense reported.

Compassion

Every case is treated as a person first, a statistic second.

Accountability

We answer to our donors first, and we report accordingly.

Measurable Impact

We track outcomes, not just dollars spent.
