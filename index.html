FAQ

Common questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?+

Tax treatment depends on your country of residence and our registration status there. We'll confirm what documentation applies to you at checkout.

How do I know a campaign is real?+

Every campaign is verified on the ground by our team before it's published, and each one includes updates, documents, and a gallery as it progresses.

Can I cancel a monthly donation anytime?+

Yes. Monthly donations can be paused or cancelled at any time from your account settings, no questions asked.

How much of my donation reaches the cause?+

86% of donations go directly to program delivery. The full breakdown is on our Financial Transparency page.
