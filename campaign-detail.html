Emergency Surgeries for Children

About this campaign

Every year, hundreds of children in Pakistan need surgery their families simply cannot afford — congenital heart conditions, severe burns, orthopedic injuries. This fund covers the full cost of pre-approved procedures at our partner hospitals in Lahore, Multan, and Karachi, from diagnosis through post-operative care.

Each case is reviewed by our medical panel before approval, and families receive support without ever seeing a bill.
