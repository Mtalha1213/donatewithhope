Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Donate With Hope Foundation ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our donors and everyone who contacts us. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you contact us or subscribe to our newsletter.

Donation Information

Donations are processed through our third-party payment partner. We do not collect or store your card or payment details ourselves — that information is handled entirely by the payment provider under its own privacy and security terms.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries sent through our contact form or email
  • To send updates, if you've subscribed to our newsletter
  • To meet legal and regulatory reporting requirements

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect any personal information you share with us and do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from email updates at any time, and can request a copy or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@donatewithhope.org.
