Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Donate With Hope Foundation ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our donors, volunteers, and beneficiaries. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email, phone number, and payment details when you donate, volunteer, or contact us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process donations and send confirmation receipts
  • To send impact updates, if you opt in
  • To respond to inquiries and volunteer applications
  • To meet legal and regulatory reporting requirements

Data Protection

We use industry-standard encryption and access controls to protect your data. Payment information is processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways and is never stored on our own servers.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from email updates at any time, and can request a copy or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@donatewithhope.org.
